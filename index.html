03 / Independent research

Recurrent models & GPU systems

Independent research · PyTorch, JAX, C++/CUDA & Triton

Investigating structured memory through implementations that can be checked against reference behaviour.

I implement recurrent models and GPU scan kernels, connect them to training code, and write comparisons for numerical outputs, gradients and streaming execution.

Model implementation
Structured state-space models and parallel scans for recurrent state updates.
GPU integration
Custom kernels and autograd integration for forward and backward execution.
Verification
Reference comparisons, gradient checks and controlled benchmarks to examine correctness and performance.
Explore the research code ↗
Engineering details

The wider research includes equivariant operators, noncommutative recurrence and exact-arithmetic checks with SymPy/FLINT. Artifact-integrity tooling records and checks research outputs. This is independent experimental work; the links expose the implementations and checks.

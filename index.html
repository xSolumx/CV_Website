Independent research

Recurrent models
& GPU systems

  • PyTorch
  • CUDA / Triton
  • JAX
Explore research code ↗

Exploring structured recurrence through model implementations and explicit checks.

  • Recurrent state-space models, parallel scans and custom autograd integration.
  • Reference comparisons for numerical outputs, training gradients and streaming equivalence.
  • Controlled benchmarks, exact-arithmetic checks and research-artifact verification.
Implementation notes

The work includes PyTorch and JAX/Flax models, C++/CUDA and Triton kernels, and SymPy/FLINT checks. Research questions span structured memory, equivariant operators and noncommutative recurrence.

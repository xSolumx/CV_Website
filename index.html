03 / Mobile & environmental data

Agronomy field-record application

In development · React Native, Expo, SQLite & Fastify

An agronomy application for recording rainfall and temperature, keeping corrections, and consulting forecasts alongside field observations.

I developed the mobile record workflows, local persistence and server synchronisation, alongside weather-data validation, caching and visualisation.

Records can be saved without a connection and uploaded later. Corrections retain their history; missing readings stay distinct from zero rainfall.

Data handling & development status

SQLite records double as a durable upload queue. Stable identifiers support retries, dependencies keep corrections after their original records, and conflicts remain visible. TypeScript contracts connect the Expo app to Fastify and PostgreSQL services.

Open-Meteo forecasts are validated and cached with source and freshness information. Failed refreshes preserve previous data. The application is in development; hosted rollout, hardware integration and trained forecasting models are outside the demonstrated scope.

04 / Applied AI & document engineering

From PDF to a book you can hear

In development · Python, OCR, speech alignment & EPUB

An authoring pipeline for turning PDFs into EPUBs with recorded or generated narration and synchronised text highlighting.

I developed document-conversion components, text-fragment processing, narration alignment and EPUB media-overlay packaging. The reader supports previewing the output; the conversion pipeline is the centre of the project.

Conversion, narration & development status

Fixed-layout and reflowable conversion paths use PDF extraction and optional OCR. Existing recordings follow a Whisper-family transcription and alignment path; generated narration uses Edge TTS. Fragment identifiers and SMIL timing references connect text passages to embedded audio.

The components are implemented, with integration and validation still in progress. Layout preservation, alignment quality and playback compatibility are active development work.

05 / Independent research

How should a model remember?

Research engineering · PyTorch, JAX, CUDA & Triton

I investigate how neural models retain useful information, learn structure and execute efficiently.

I implement experimental model families, learned-memory mechanisms and the training and evaluation code around them. I also build GPU kernels and model-specific execution plans to study the cost of running those ideas.

Matched baselines and reference checks help separate model behaviour from implementation effects. I compare learning quality, runtime and memory use, and document where an approach helps and where it does not.

Explore the research code ↗
Architectures, execution & evidence

The monorepo contains separate programmes for associative scans, learned memory, equivariant models and controlled benchmarks. Implementations include CUDA/Triton kernels, custom autograd and shape- and precision-aware dispatch. Output and gradient checks compare optimised paths with references; pinned data and artifact manifests keep results traceable. Fused gathered-memory inference is evaluated separately from trainable model paths.
